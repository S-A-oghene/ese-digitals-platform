What Are You Made Of? From Job Searching to Opportunity Intelligence

By Ese Digitals · Canonical website article · September 2026

Searching for work can become an exercise in collecting links, applications and rejections. But a deeper question sits underneath the search: is the opportunity genuine, current, relevant and realistically pursueable?

The problem is bigger than search

Opportunity intelligence combines discovery with eligibility, verification, freshness, relevance and prioritisation. A job being labelled “remote” does not automatically make it worldwide or eligible for every country.

From queries to intelligence

The Ese Digitals Opportunity Intelligence Engine begins with structured search intent and is designed to move toward a fuller intelligence pipeline: discovery, normalization, evidence, eligibility, matching, ranking and action.

Capability matters too

I do not think people should stop applying for jobs. I also do not think a person's ability to create value should depend entirely on whether an employer says yes. The larger question is what capability can be developed, what problems can be solved and what evidence can be built along the way.

What are you made of?

Maybe the point is not to know the final answer immediately. Maybe it is to keep refining: keep learning, keep solving, keep building, keep applying, keep experimenting and keep creating evidence.
